# Knowledge Score

Knowledge score version: `knowledge-score-v1`.

The score is an admin ranking aid only. It does not authorize processing.

Components:

- identity completeness
- authorization coverage
- provenance quality
- corpus volume
- approved-pair coverage
- single-service coverage
- pattern consistency
- map-definition coverage
- human-verification depth
- validation depth
- conflict penalty
- quarantine penalty
- synthetic-data penalty

Hard blockers are displayed and reduce the score. A high numeric score cannot override a hard blocker.
